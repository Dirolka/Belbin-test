# Simple Interest Calculator

A simple interest calculator that calculates the interest earned or paid on a principal amount over a specified period at a fixed annual interest rate.

## Inputs

- **Principal (P):** The initial amount invested or borrowed.
- **Annual interest rate (R):** The annual interest rate expressed as a percentage.
- **Time (T):** The duration in years.

## Formula

```text
Simple Interest = (P × R × T) / 100
Total Amount = P + Simple Interest
```

Simple interest is calculated only on the original principal and does not include compound interest.

## Example

For a principal of 1,000, an annual interest rate of 5%, and a duration of 2 years:

```text
Simple Interest = (1000 × 5 × 2) / 100 = 100
Total Amount = 1000 + 100 = 1100
```

## Output

The calculator returns the simple interest for the specified period.
